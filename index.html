Hours & Heroes – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Hours & Heroes is built as an offline-first productivity and RPG application. Your privacy is simple and absolute: we do not collect, store, track, or share any of your personal data.

1. No Data Collection

Hours & Heroes does not collect any personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data. The app requires no user account, no login, and no internet connection to function.

2. Local On-Device Storage Only

All data generated while using the app—including your daily schedule, focus session history, hero progression, camp upgrades, and app preferences—is stored strictly locally on your device. We have no external servers and cannot access your data. If you delete the app from your device, all locally stored game data is permanently removed.

3. In-App Purchases

Hours & Heroes offers a 2-day free trial followed by an optional one-time, non-consumable In-App Purchase to unlock the full game forever. All payment transactions and purchase restorations are processed directly and securely by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We never receive, process, or store your billing details, Apple ID, or credit card information.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising

The app contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising networks, and zero tracking scripts.

5. Children’s Privacy

Because Hours & Heroes does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information from any user, it is completely safe and compliant with global privacy regulations for users of all ages.
